🔏 Redaction Proof Verifier

Independent, offline verification of a Privacy-Vision-Agent redaction proof. This page needs no server, no extension, and no network connection — it only needs the proof JSON. It never sees the original screenshot; it mathematically confirms the proof's internal consistency using nothing but hashes and a public key, both already embedded in the proof itself.


What "valid" means here: every tile the extension claims was redacted really was part of the single screenshot it originally committed to (its Merkle inclusion proof recomputes to the signed root), and the whole manifest is signed by the key embedded in the proof — so nothing in this JSON was edited after the fact. What it does NOT mean: this does not prove who ran the extension or that the key belongs to any particular person/organization — the signing key is generated fresh per browsing session specifically so this tool proves internal pipeline consistency, not signer identity. It also does not reveal, and cannot reveal, what was in any redacted region — SHA-256 hashes do not leak their input.
